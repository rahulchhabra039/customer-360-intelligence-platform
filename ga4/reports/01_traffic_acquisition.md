# GA4 traffic acquisition analysis

## Purpose

Determine which channel groups bring website sessions and which produce engaged, revenue-generating traffic.

## Property and period

- Property: Google Merchandise Store demo property
- Period viewed: 2026-07-25 to 2026-08-21
- Primary dimension: Session primary channel group

## Overall results

- Sessions: 110,584
- Engaged sessions: 43,389
- Engagement rate: 39.24%
- Average engagement time per session: 43 seconds
- Events per session: 12.14
- Key events: 94,440
- Session key-event rate: 21.32%
- Total revenue: $196,106.44

## Necessary findings

| Channel | Session share | Engagement rate | Revenue share | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Direct | 63.06% | 25.94% | 55.05% | Highest scale, but relatively weak engagement |
| Organic Search | 21.84% | 72.14% | 32.00% | Strongest scalable quality channel |
| Unassigned | 5.38% | 12.78% | 1.67% | Likely attribution or tagging-quality issue |
| Paid Search | 4.43% | 59.34% | 4.83% | Reasonable, balanced contribution |
| Referral | 1.67% | 72.77% | 3.85% | Small but valuable audience |

## Business recommendation

Protect and strengthen Organic Search because it combines material scale with strong engagement and revenue contribution. Investigate Unassigned traffic and campaign tagging. Analyse high-quality referral sources before increasing investment.
